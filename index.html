How an order runs

Process

You get an update at the end of each step, so there are no surprises on delivery day.

Step 1

Brief

We agree on the goal, the metric that matters to you, and the deadline.

Step 2

Data check

I look for missing values, class imbalance and leaks before any training.

Step 3

Model

A simple baseline first, then stronger models compared on the same folds.

Step 4

Explain

SHAP, calibration and error analysis, so you know where the model is weak.

Step 5

Deliver

Code, model, report and, if ordered, a live app. One round of revisions included.
